## Why this portfolio

It is built from the same funds and with the same method as the model portfolios, adapted to my
own situation.

**A fixed-income cushion.** A quarter of the portfolio is in short-duration bonds and defensive
alternatives (Neuberger Berman, Lazard, Gamma Global, Helium and Fermat). It is there to fit the
portfolio to the risk I want to take and to my time horizon.

**A tilt towards Europe.** Alken Small Cap Europe adds small and mid-sized European companies, a
segment the global index fund barely covers.

**A bet on John Tidd.** Hamco Global Value follows John Tidd's deep value approach: the cheapest
companies in any market. It sits next to Cobas and Azvalor in the value part of the portfolio.

**A little of frontier markets.** Redwheel Next Generation Emerging Markets invests in smaller and
less developed markets than the usual emerging-market indices. At 2.5 % it is the smallest weight.

**The rest is close to the model portfolios:** Fidelity MSCI World as the core, Cobas and Azvalor
for value, Robeco for emerging markets, and Jupiter and BlackRock as absolute-return alternatives.
